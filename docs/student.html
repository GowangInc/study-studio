Keep Study Studio handy

You can work in the browser or add Study Studio to your device. On a Chromebook, Windows, or Android device, look for Install app in the browser menu. On an iPad or iPhone, open Study Studio in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen. On a Mac, you can use Safari’s File → Add to Dock.

Your teacher opens the workspace during class. Add sources to a project, then choose the ones you want to use in your requests. Save your files before the session ends. You can download and open them on your own device.
